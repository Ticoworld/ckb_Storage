# WO02 — Shared CKB state at high native prices

**Decision: PIVOT TO PROTOCOL ECONOMICS.** The minimal type-enforced experiment
works under its stated failure assumptions, but does not establish a competitive
economic advantage that justifies expanding the new pool.

142 real-validator/resolver outcomes pass (110 accepts, 32 expected rejections).
All 24 four-user withdrawal orders preserve funding. A separate client updates
its retained proof from public packed transactions and produces an accepted
exit. Real ACP accepts a small existing-cell credit; the fresh standard-cell
route still fails capacity. No live devnet/mainnet, production light client or
permanent data-availability guarantee is claimed.
